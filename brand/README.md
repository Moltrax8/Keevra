# Keevra Brand Kit

Identity: warm paper, ink, one persimmon accent, and a Palatino/Georgia serif
wordmark — the same system the app uses (`src/App.css`).

## Contents

- `01_App_Icons/` — the 3D app icon, recolored to persimmon, in dark and light
  variants and nine PNG sizes (16–1024).
- `02_Wordmarks/` — horizontal and stacked serif wordmarks, dark and light, on
  the app's paper backgrounds.
- `04_Windows/` — multi-resolution ICO files and a high-resolution PNG,
  generated from the recolored 3D artwork.

## Notes

- The app icons are the original illustrated 3D renders recolored to the
  persimmon palette; the shading, bevels and highlights are preserved.
- The light source crop was missing the bottom of its square tile; the tile is
  completed by continuing its own surface gradient, then masked to a rounded
  square with a transparent surround.
- Wordmarks are typeset in Palatino Linotype Bold, matching the app's display
  serif.
- At 16–32 px the 3D detail collapses to a small persimmon symbol; review icon
  legibility in the actual Windows taskbar before release.
